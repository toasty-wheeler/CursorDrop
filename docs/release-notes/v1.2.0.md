Schedule your cursors: different ones on weekdays and weekends, per week of the month, and on special days every year.

**Download `CursorDrop-v1.2.0-win64.zip`**, exit the running CursorDrop (tray icon > Exit), unzip it over your old CursorDrop folder (or anywhere) and run `CursorDrop.exe`. Your packs, presets and settings carry over.

### New: Schedule
Click **Schedule** in the window's header.

- **Weeks of the month** (no dates; it repeats every month): week 1 is the 1st–7th, week 2 the 8th–14th, up to week 5. Tap days to select them (as many as you like) or use **Weekdays**, **Weekends**, **Every day**. The **+** under the weekend adds a week (up to 5); with fewer weeks they take turns within the month.
- **For the selected days** pick **One** set of cursors, a **Random pick** from several you tick, **Random from all**, or **No change**. Pack variants and presets can both be used.
- **Special days**: pick a date in the date picker (a holiday, a birthday…). It comes back every year, whatever weekday it falls on, and wins over the weeks.
- Under the weeks, the days are listed like a calendar app's events: "Weekdays", "Weekends", your special dates, with what each gets and a **Today** marker.
- Turn on **Follow the schedule** (on the page or in the tray menu): each new day gets its cursors, and a random pick stays the same for the whole day.

SHA-256 of the zip is in `CursorDrop-v1.2.0-win64.zip.sha256`.
