Choose any cursor yourself, and save your own presets.

**Download `CursorDrop-v1.1.0-win64.zip`**, exit the running CursorDrop (tray icon > Exit), unzip it over your old CursorDrop folder (or anywhere) and run `CursorDrop.exe`. Your packs and settings carry over.

### New
- **Pick any cursor by hand.** Click a cursor in a pack's grid to choose a different one: any cursor in the pack (grouped by folder, with likely matches marked), a cursor from another pack or preset, **Keep current**, or **Windows default**. Great for packs whose file names don't say which cursor is which. Changed cursors are marked **Edited**, **Reset changes** puts the pack back, and your choices are used when you click **Use these cursors**.
- **Presets.** **Current cursors**, always first in the list, shows what Windows uses right now. Change any of its cursors, or a pack's, and **Save as preset…** (or **Save and use**). A preset copies its cursor files, so it keeps working even if the packs it came from are removed. Presets can be used, changed, renamed and deleted, and are in the tray menu too.
- **Better file-name matching**: names like `accuracy`, `stop`, `width`, `height`, `ne`/`nw`, `alternative`, and packs that call their normal pointer just `<name>.cur` (next to `<name>_busy.ani` and so on) are now recognised. Packs you already have are re-read once on first start.

SHA-256 of the zip is in `CursorDrop-v1.1.0-win64.zip.sha256`.
